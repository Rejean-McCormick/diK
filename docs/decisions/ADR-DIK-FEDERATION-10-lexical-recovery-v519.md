# ADR 10 — Lexical recovery without implicit promotion

Status: local candidate. Preserve the active V11 catalog and external alignment
statuses. Index historical scoped terms and cached Wikidata forms as private
observations; retain provenance and competing variants. Only a previously
accepted exact alignment may anchor a cached Q/P. No cross-language translation,
legacy ID inference or independent approval is performed. Use the 14 locales
from scan14.py as a reporting subset; KA/KS are not lexical dictionary entries.
Source catalog declarations of checksum verification are recorded, not upgraded.
Qualification and canonical promotion remain separate Authoring Kit operations.
